## 2. What Dad Lore is

Dad Lore helps you find nearby activities worth turning into a story, and
keep a private log of the ones you've actually done. Activity listings —
including ones tagged "Certified Bad Ideas" — are meant to be fun and a
little dumb, not a set of instructions we're telling you to follow.

supervise, inspect, or guarantee the safety of any activity, location, or
listing. You're responsible for using good judgment, following the law,
respecting private property, and not doing anything that puts yourself or
anyone else in real danger. **Participate at your own risk.**

## 4. Your content

Anything you submit (a report, and in the future, submitted lore) must
follow the community guidelines: no harassment, no hate speech, no sexual
content, and no genuinely dangerous or illegal activity presented as a
serious suggestion. We can remove content or restrict access for
violations.
